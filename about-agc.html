Our Story & Heritage

"One Advisor. Every Financial Decision of Your Life."

Aarya Group, under the leadership of Chief Mentor Sanjay K. Gupta, is a consortium of entrepreneurs offering a comprehensive range of financial, investment, accounting, auditing, estate planning, and legal services to clients in India and internationally for over two decades.

Our team includes 42 associates, backed by a highly experienced and qualified group of Financial Consultants, Chartered Accountants, Attorneys, Lawyers, and Advocates. Together, we provide tailored solutions to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

With our headquarters in Kolkata, we maintain a pan-India presence across Mumbai, Bengaluru, the entire NCR, Siliguri, Guwahati, Patna, and Ranchi.

Guided by our philosophy, 'Let's Grow Together', we focus on overall growth in health, wealth, and happiness for our clients and ourselves. Over 2,000 clients in India and abroad have placed their trust in us.
