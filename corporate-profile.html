Aarya's Intervention: Consolidated into a streamlined 9-scheme multi-asset portfolio, deployed tax-loss harvesting to neutralize past gains, and transitioned to an institutional fiduciary advisory mandate.

Cross-Border / NRI · Estate & DTAA Structuring
Cross-Border Succession & Double-Tax Shielding for UAE-Based NRI Family
The Challenge: NRI family holding substantial commercial properties and equity portfolios in Kolkata faced complex inheritance laws, potential double-taxation across UAE-India, and probate delays.
Aarya's Intervention: Created a private irrevocable family trust holding Indian equity assets, drafted compliant cross-border wills, and aligned DTAA tax residency certificates to eliminate withholding tax disputes.
Outcome: 100% dispute-free estate transition roadmapped; legal probate time reduced to zero through private trust structure.
