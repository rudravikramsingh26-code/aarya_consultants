Financial Planning

Six Steps Financial planning process to clearly understand and quantify the needs and wants to curate a customised financial plan for better wealth management.

Our Foundational Standard

Advice First.
Products Second.

We start with your investment pattern, income, expenses, assets, and liabilities then quantify what you actually need, by when. Only after that do we discuss products.

Where you already hold a portfolio, we review it independently: what it costs you, what it is likely to return, where the concentration risk sits, and what should change. Our philosophy is simple: act as a fiduciary, and the relationship takes care of itself.

Disciplined Roadmap

The Six-Step Financial Planning Discipline

Our standardized process follows globally recognized fiduciary standards to ensure that nothing is left to chance.

Step 01

Establish Relationship

Defining client responsibilities, advisor commitments, fiduciary standards, and mutual expectations clearly upfront.

Step 02

Gather Data & Goals

Mapping all incomes, outgoings, existing policies, mutual fund holdings, real estate, debts, and timeline aspirations.

Step 03

Analyze & Evaluate

Calculating financial ratios, post-tax yields, inflation impact, and identifying shortfalls in retirement or protection.

Step 04

Curate Recommendations

Developing customized asset allocation blueprints with product rationale documented with complete transparency.

Step 05

Implement Solutions

Seamless execution across SIPs, mutual funds, protection covers, debt structuring, and tax-efficient instruments.

Step 06

Monitor & Review

Regular performance reviews, annual portfolio rebalancing, and adapting the roadmap as life and markets evolve.
