Our Offerings

Products curated across India’s leading institutions, independent of sales targets and aligned strictly to your goals.

Life Insurance

Protect your family’s financial future with life insurance solutions tailored to your needs. Aarya Consultants assesses your human life value, income, liabilities, and dependants to recommend suitable term, traditional, or unit-linked plans that provide meaningful, long-term security.

Health Insurance

Secure your health and your family’s wellbeing with insurance plans tailored to your needs. We offer individual and family floater policies, super top-up covers, and senior citizen plans, backed by personalized guidance and dependable claim settlement support when it matters most.

General Insurance

Protect your assets, enterprise, and journeys. Comprehensive policies covering vehicle, residential, commercial office, godown fire covers, professional indemnity, export-import marine transit, and international travel health covers matched to your risk profile.

Mutual Funds & NFOs

Build and grow your wealth with goal-oriented mutual fund and New Fund Offer (NFO) solutions. We select suitable AMFI-registered schemes through disciplined SIPs and lump-sum investments, backed by regular quarterly portfolio reviews and timely rebalancing.

Equity Broking & IPOs

Access the equity markets with seamless investment support. We assist with Demat and trading account facilitation, IPO applications, and transaction execution, helping investors navigate market opportunities with informed guidance and disciplined risk control.

FDs, Bonds & Debentures

Strengthen your portfolio with reliable fixed-income investment options. Access corporate and NBFC fixed deposits, sovereign and PSU bonds, and rated debentures to balance risk, preserve capital, and secure predictable coupon returns.

PPF, Post Office & NPS

Secure long-term goals with Public Provident Fund and Post Office scheme advisory. We guide you through government-backed savings instruments designed for disciplined investing, sovereign capital security, tax-efficient deductions, and predictable maturity corpus.

Loans & Financing

Access competitive financing solutions. We assist with home loans, business loans, loan-against-property (LAP), and loan-against-securities (LAS) through our network of partner banks and NBFCs, helping you compare interest rates and secure streamlined funding.

Retirement & Goal Planning

Plan confidently for retirement and life’s important milestones. We estimate required retirement corpus, design structured monthly drawdown strategies, and recommend balanced asset allocations including gold where appropriate for inflation resilience.

SIFs, PMS & AIFs

SIF, PMS & AIF solutions built around your wealth goals. Customized Portfolio Management Services (PMS), Alternative Investment Funds (AIF Cat I, II & III), and high-potential structured debentures designed for high-net-worth investors and family offices.

Real Estate Investment

Real estate investment that builds lasting wealth. Strategic advisory for residential property investments, commercial real estate opportunities, REITs, long-term capital appreciation, and stable rental income structuring.

GIFT City for NRIs

Global investment opportunities through Gujarat International Finance Tec-City (GIFT City). Access foreign currency-denominated instruments, global funds, and NRI-friendly tax frameworks with simplified cross-border compliance.
