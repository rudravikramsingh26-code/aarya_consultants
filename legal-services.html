Discreet Fiduciary Standard

Planning Wealth Without Planning Its Transfer Is Only Half the Job.

Most disputes within families are not about money. They are about ambiguity: an unwritten intention, an unregistered property, a nominee who was never meant to be the owner, a business with no succession line.

Aarya Estate Planners and Legal Services was created because our financial clients kept arriving at the same question: what happens to all of this afterwards? Our attorneys, lawyers, and advocates work alongside the same team that manages your investments and your books, so the plan on paper matches the assets in reality. Every engagement is strictly confidential. Documents are handled by named professionals, not passed around a team.

Practice Areas

How We Help

Comprehensive estate structuring, cross-generation business transfer, and statutory registration under one roof.

Estate Planning

A complete inventory of what you own, how each asset is titled, and how it should pass on. We align nominations, ownership, and tax implications so they never contradict each other.

  • Asset & liability register
  • Title & nomination audit
  • Stamp duty & transfer tax roadmap

Succession Planning

Continuity for the family business and personal wealth across generations. For a business, succession is a management problem before it is legal: who runs it, who owns it, and who is compensated.

  • Business leadership transition
  • Next-gen onboarding & roles
  • Avoiding probate delay & disputes

Will Building (5 Steps)

A will is the simplest and most powerful instrument—and the one most often written badly. We draft, execute, witness, and safely store wills properly, and remind you to review every 3 years.

  • Asset listing & beneficiaries
  • Residuary clauses & executor
  • Witnessing & safe custody

Family Trust

Separates ownership from benefit. Private structures for protection, control, and orderly distribution of assets: ideal for special-needs dependents, minor children, and business ring-fencing.

  • Irrevocable & revocable deeds
  • Trustee appointment & briefing
  • Ongoing trust compliance

Society & Trust Formation

Registration and governance for societies, charitable trusts, associations, and Section 8 companies. We handle deed drafting, PAN, TAN, 12A, 80G, FCRA, and statutory NPO audits.

  • Society & trust deed registration
  • 12A, 80G & FCRA certifications
  • NPO statutory audit & compliance

Legal & Property Services

Property title search, sale deed drafting, registration, land mutation, consumer forum litigation, commercial contract vetting, and general civil legal advisory.

  • Property registration & mutation
  • Consumer court & civil matters
  • Commercial contract negotiation

Proven Precedents

Situations We Have Resolved

Real scenarios from our two decades of fiduciary estate and legal practice in India.

Case 01 · Nomination vs Heirship

The Unnamed Nominee

A family assumed the nominee on a fixed deposit and mutual fund holding was the permanent legal owner. Correcting the nomination, executing gift instruments, and drafting a clear will prevented a bitter legal dispute between three siblings.

Case 02 · Family Enterprise

The Business With No Line

A founder in his sixties with two children in different professions. Creating a private family trust plus a documented family settlement agreement kept the core commercial enterprise intact while both heirs were fairly and amicably compensated.

Case 03 · Joint Property Titling

The Property Nobody Could Sell

An inherited residential property held jointly across four heirs with no formal mutation or partition deed. Our legal team completed title cleanup, succession paperwork, and registrar mutation in under four months, allowing clean liquidity.
