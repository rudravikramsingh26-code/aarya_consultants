Tax Practice

Our Taxation Services

Year-round legitimate tax planning, filing compliance, and scrutiny support across individuals, corporate firms, and cross-border NRI estates.

Income Tax Return Filing

Salaried, business, capital gains, foreign income, and NRI returns prepared with meticulous calculation and complete working papers retained for you.

Tax Planning

Legitimate, documented tax planning across the year rather than a scramble in March. Advance tax computation, timing reviews, and payment scheduling.

GST Compliance

Registration, monthly (GSTR-1/3B) and quarterly returns, annual return (GSTR-9/9C) reconciliation, input tax credit (ITC) audit, and refund claims.

TDS Compliance

Monthly deduction working, government challan payment, quarterly returns (24Q/26Q), and timely Form 16 / 16A generation and issuance.

PF, ESI & Payroll

Registration, monthly employee contributions, statutory returns, and complete employee-level compliance records maintained to audit readiness.

Entity Formation

Incorporation of Private Limited companies, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), partnerships, proprietorships, trusts, and Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs).

Licences & Registrations

Trade licence, food licence (FSSAI), MSME / Udyam registration, professional tax (P-Tax), and Shops & Establishments Act compliance.

ROC & Corporate Compliance

Annual company filing (AOC-4, MGT-7), director KYC (DIR-3 KYC), auditor appointments (ADT-1), and event-based MCA statutory filings.

Notices, Assessments & Appeals

Response drafting, evidence collation, and representation before tax authorities for scrutiny notices, demand rectifications, and appellate matters.

Export & Import Benefits

Advisory and claim facilitation for RoDTEP, RoSCTL, duty drawback, and customs incentive schemes for commercial export-import clients.

Cross-Border & NRI Taxation

Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) optimization, foreign asset reporting (Schedule FA), 15CA/CB remittances, and capital gains on Indian real estate sales.

Never Miss a Due Date

Monthly, quarterly, and annual obligations—TDS, GST, PF, advance tax, and ROC audit milestones—tracked and managed with proactive alerts.

Old Regime or New Regime — How to Decide?

A plain-language comparison calibrated to your actual deductions, HRA, home loan interest, and business expenses. Refreshed each assessment year so your tax strategy is always optimized for minimum liability.
