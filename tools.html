    Cash-flow sign convention

    PURCHASE, SIP and SWITCH_IN are negative investor cash flows. REDEMPTION, SWITCH_OUT and DIVIDEND are positive investor cash flows. Enter each transaction amount as a positive number; the engine applies the sign. For switches, record both legs. Do not count an internal switch as an external portfolio contribution or withdrawal in a whole-portfolio XIRR: the paired legs should net to zero.
